What is QEV, in plain words?

QEV helps you save a clear record of what you approved — so later you can prove it was that file or that AI model version, not “something with a similar name.”

Try the demo See the steps

Runs in your browser. This demo is not trying to upload your files to us.

Why anyone would care

Teams say “we approved that AI model.” Months later, nobody is sure:

  • Which version did we actually look at?
  • What evidence did we have?
  • What did we allow or block?
  • Did the model change after we said yes?

QEV’s job: put that answer in one package you can re-check later.

What you get

A downloadable package (like a sealed folder) that includes: the thing you reviewed, the notes you chose to attach, the decision, and a way to check if anything changed.

The business version (AI models)

Same idea, for outside AI models your team wants to use:

  • Lock the exact version (not just a product name)
  • Keep public docs with the record
  • Keep private test notes private (sealed inside)
  • Record the decision: no / limited use / later approval
  • If a new version shows up later, the old approval does not silently transfer

Demo examples: one model is rejected (not enough proof), another is “sandbox only” (limited use).

Please don’t over-read this demo.
It does not mean the AI is safe or unsafe. It does not mean this is finished enterprise software. It means: “here is a clear, checkable record of what was decided about this exact version.”

Who this is for

  • Teams that use AI models from the internet
  • People who get asked later: “what exactly did we approve?”
  • Anyone tired of answers living only in email and chat
