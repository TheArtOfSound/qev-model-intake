The problem

“We already approved that model” is not a record.

Outside models enter the building quickly: open downloads, vendor systems, agent tools. The “yes” usually lives in email, Slack, or a wiki page. That works until someone asks a hard question after the fact.

Which version did you review? What evidence did you have? What was allowed or blocked? If the model changed under the same name, does yesterday’s approval still count?

If you cannot hand someone a single package that answers those questions, you do not have model intake. You have hope.

What goes wrong

  • Names hide version changes
  • Docs, notes, and decisions live in different tools
  • Private testing cannot be pasted into a public model page
  • Updates arrive quietly; old approvals stick around

What people try instead

  • Long chat threads that nobody can find later
  • PDFs that cannot be checked machine-side
  • A registry entry that never leaves the platform
  • “Trust the host” without an internal decision trail

How it works

A simple path from review to a package you can keep.

QEV does not need to replace the place you download models or the system where you track tickets. It produces the approval package that sits next to the work.

  1. Start with what you care about An outside AI model, or another important package you need to approve.
  2. Lock the exact version Not a brand name—the specific version your people actually reviewed.
  3. Attach the evidence you used Public materials stay public. Private notes can stay sealed inside the package.
  4. Record the decision and keep the package No, limited use, or a path you define. Later, open it and check that it still matches.

Who this is for

People who already feel the mess.

  • AI or platform lead

    Teams pull models into prototypes every week. You need a light process that still leaves a trail when something becomes real.

  • Security

    Third-party models are part of your supply chain. You want a clear package for review without standing up another platform project.

  • Risk or compliance partner

    When someone asks what was approved, you need a record—not a scavenger hunt across five tools.

  • Research and public-sector partners

    Same operational need in dual-use settings: bind decisions to exact assets without selling a magic safety score.

How teams adopt it

A small pilot, on models you already care about.

We are not asking anyone to “rip and replace.” The useful path is a short pilot: a handful of real models, one clear approval policy, and packages your stakeholders can open.

Straight talk

What this is not.

We will not sell you a fairy tale

  • QEV does not score whether a model is “safe.”
  • QEV does not guarantee a supplier’s public documents are true—it records what you used.
  • Your organization still owns testing and the final call.
  • We do not claim finished enterprise-suite status on day one.

That honesty is the product. People adopt tools they can explain without wincing.
